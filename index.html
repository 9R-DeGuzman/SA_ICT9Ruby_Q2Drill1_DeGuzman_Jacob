Here's how to play Dodgeball


Dodgeball is a team sport where two groups aim to eliminate players from their opposing team by throwing balls at them while simultaneously avoiding being hit.

General Basis
  • You get the balls from the right side of your team
  • Getting hit means you are out. Headshots are not counted
  • The game ends once every player in one team gets eliminated
Tips on how to win
  • Hit as hard as you can. The ball goes further the more you charge up your throw, making it's trajectory more accurate
  • Don't just run away from the ball. Use your body to make the ball go through you
Enjoyment and Origins
  • People love the thrill of hitting their opponents without any malicious intent
  • It originated in Africa and was later transformed as a team sport in the US and England
